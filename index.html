Who
We Are

Frame Studio is a creative design agency operating out of Maputo, Mozambique. We specialize in avoiding templates and shortcuts to craft assets entirely from scratch. Every design decision combines an international mindset with a deep local understanding, driven by strategic thinking and fast, transparent turnarounds.
